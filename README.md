# STELLA System Dynamics: Helge Å Water Allocation

## Project Overview
Developed a System Dynamics model in STELLA Architect to evaluate water management trade-offs in the Helge Å basin under future climate scenarios (2031-2050).

## Model Structure
*   **Hydrology:** Lake Volume stock with inputs from direct precipitation and catchment runoff (based on the 1,500 km² GIS-delineated catchment).
*   **Climate Data:** Integrated real SMHI precipitation and temperature data via Graphical Functions.
*   **Economics & Ecology:** Calculated profits for Hydropower and Irrigation, alongside tourism revenue for the Ramsar-protected bird sanctuary.

## Sensitivity Analysis & Results
*   Tested various lake outflow constants (\(K_m\)) and management switches.
*   **Conclusion:** Identified the "Goldilocks Zone" (lake depth 1.1m - 1.8m) required to sustain both economic development and ecosystem protection.
